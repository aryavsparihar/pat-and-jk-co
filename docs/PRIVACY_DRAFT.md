## What we collect, and why

**When you write to us** through a form: your name, email, and what you tell us. To answer you.

**When you apply as an artist or partner:** your email, name, date of birth, phone number,
address, and what you tell us about your work, with pictures of it. If you choose to, a pin on a
map showing where you live. The map is Google's (or OpenStreetMap's), and it loads only if you open
it; then that company's servers see your internet address, and Google also sees what you type in
the map's address search and the address already in the form, to find it on the map. On
OpenStreetMap's map, only the town, state and country you typed are sent to OpenStreetMap's place
search, to open the map there, and if you choose Satellite or Terrain, Esri's or OpenTopoMap's
servers also see your internet address. If you press "Use my location", your browser asks you
first, and your device's position is used only to place the pin; it is kept only if you save the
pin with the step. To consider your application and to work
with you if we approve it.

**Identity details (artists).** Your PAN and the name on it, which we ask of every artist. A second
ID showing your address: a masked e-Aadhaar with its last four digits, or, if you would rather, a
passport, voter ID or driving licence. A proof of address if that address differs from the one you
gave. A photo of your PAN card, on which your face must be clearly visible. A photo of your face,
taken in the page with your camera or uploaded by you, holding a paper on which you have written a
code we give you, your full name and your date of birth, so we know a real person is applying and
that it is you. We compare the face in it with the photograph on your PAN card and your ID; we do
not use it for anything else, and we do not use automated face recognition. The code lasts ten
minutes; we keep it only as a one-way hash until the photo is taken, and then, encrypted, with the
photo. **[confirm with counsel: collecting face photographs for identity checks]** To be sure of
who you are, and to keep the records the law requires when we pay you. We ask for your separate
consent to this, after you have read this policy to its end, and we record when you consented, to
which version of this policy, and that you typed I AGREE to confirm it. We use these details for
nothing else.

**Your agreement to our terms (artists).** When you send your application we keep a record that you
agreed to our Terms & Conditions, what you typed to confirm it, when, and which version.

**Payout details (artists we work with).** Your bank account: the account holder's name, the
account number, the IFSC and the branch. We collect these only when a sale needs a payout, not
when you apply. We ask for them by email, but you add them only in your account on this website,
after a code we send you; we never ask for bank details by email and never accept them that way.
We send ₹1 to the account to check it before the first payment. We use them to pay you and to
keep the records the law requires, and for nothing else.

If you give us a masked e-Aadhaar and its password, we use the password once, to open the file
and check UIDAI's digital signature and that the details match; we do not keep the password. You
do not have to give us Aadhaar at all. **[confirm with counsel: wording on offline verification]**

We never ask for, or keep, your full Aadhaar number.

**When you visit the site:** we count visits without cookies and without identifying you. We use
no advertising or third-party analytics. **[confirm: still true at publication]**

## How we keep it

Identity and payout details are encrypted as soon as they reach us, documents included, and shown
even to our own staff only in part (for example, the last four digits of an account). Only the
few people at PJK who check them can see them in full, and each time they do it is recorded.

We use these service providers, who process data for us and only on our instructions:

## Your rights

You can ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and what we do with it; to
correct or complete it; to delete it; and to name someone to act for you if you cannot. You can
withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by writing to us or from your
account. Withdrawing does not undo what we did before, and we may not be able to work with you or
pay you without the details.

We will answer within **[confirm: e.g. 30 days]**. If you are not satisfied, write to our
grievance officer, Mr. Yuvraj Singh Pathania. If you are still not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board
of India.

## Children

Our applications are for adults: we do not accept applications from anyone under 18, and the
form checks the date of birth.

## If something goes wrong

If your personal data is ever exposed, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as
the law requires.

We will post any change here, with the date, and ask for your consent again where the law
