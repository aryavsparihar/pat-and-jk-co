personal data you give us here, we are the data fiduciary. You can write to us about it at
**[confirm: privacy@pjk.co.in or another address]**, or by post to our registered office,
**[confirm: address as in Site settings]**. Our grievance officer is **[confirm: name and role]**.

## What we collect, and why

**When you write to us** through a form: your name, email, and what you tell us. To answer you.

**When you apply as an artist or partner:** your email, name, date of birth, phone number,
address, and what you tell us about your work, with pictures of it. To consider your application
and to work with you if we approve it.

**Identity and bank details (artists).** Your PAN and the name on it, or the last four digits of
your Aadhaar with a masked copy of the card; a proof of address if needed; and your bank account
details, with a cheque or passbook if you choose. To be sure of who you are, to pay you, and to
keep the records the law requires when we pay you. We ask for your separate consent to this, and
we use these details for nothing else.

We never ask for, or keep, your full Aadhaar number.

**When you visit the site:** we count visits without cookies and without identifying you. We use
no advertising or third-party analytics. **[confirm: still true at publication]**

## How we keep it

Identity and bank details are encrypted as soon as they reach us, documents included, and shown
even to our own staff only in part (for example, the last four digits of an account). Only the
few people at PJK who check them can see them in full, and each time they do it is recorded.

We use these service providers, who process data for us and only on our instructions:

| Neon | The database | Singapore |

Some of your data is therefore stored outside India. **[confirm with counsel: any restriction
notified under section 16 of the DPDP Act]**

We never sell your data or share it for marketing.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and what we do with it; to
correct or complete it; to delete it; and to name someone to act for you if you cannot. You can
withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by writing to us or from your
account. Withdrawing does not undo what we did before, and we may not be able to work with you or
pay you without the details.

We will answer within **[confirm: e.g. 30 days]**. If you are not satisfied, write to our
grievance officer. If you are still not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board
of India.
